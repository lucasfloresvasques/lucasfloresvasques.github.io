A telenovela brasileira em foco: uma análise sobre intimidade, emoção e processos de subjetivação em Pantanal

The Brazilian telenovela in focus: an analysis of intimacy, emotion and processes of subjectivation in Pantanal

Thaís Caetano de Souza and Lucas Flôres Vasques

Journal
EntreRios 6(1), 111–130, 2023.
DOI
10.26694/rer.v6i1.14084

Abstract

The article examines to what extent Brazilian telenovelas carry a moral and affective grammar that fosters processes of subjectivation among the Brazilians who watch them. We conducted an empirical study with a Google Forms questionnaire, circulated on social media while the final episode of Pantanal was being broadcast; the telenovela, written by Bruno Luperi and aired by TV Globo in 2022, is a remake of the 1990 original. We collected 253 questionnaires with 20 questions, focusing on emotional, affective and moral relationships with the telenovela. The data relate to the viewers' intimacy, morality and affectivity, which the characters and their stories activate, producing a kind of identification between viewer and work and between viewer and character.
