O “lugar” do liberalismo no Brasil do século XIX

The “place” of liberalism in nineteenth-century Brazil

Lucas Flôres Vasques. Revista Sem Aspas 10, e021010, 2021.

https://doi.org/10.29373/sas.v10i00.15186

Abstract

The “place” of liberalism in nineteenth-century Brazil is the subject of long-standing debate in political philosophy (Ricupero, 2007). This article maps that debate and offers a synthesis of its positions. It first returns to the context in which liberalism emerged in England and then to its philosophical principles in John Locke. It then shows how these principles were interpreted in Brazil in three positions: as a “misplaced idea” (Schwarz, 2000; Nogueira, 1976), as “an idea that tends to find its place” (Coutinho, 2000) and as an “idea in its proper place” (Franco, 1976; Bosi, 2010).
