Considerações sobre o corpo em Pierre Bourdieu e Michel Foucault

Considerations about the body in Pierre Bourdieu and Michel Foucault

Lucas Flôres Vasques and Thaís Caetano de Souza

Journal
Novos Rumos Sociológicos 11(20), 29–44, 2023. Issue dated 2023; published online in 2024.
DOI
10.15210/norus.v11i20.26234

Abstract

This essay brings together the approaches of Michel Foucault (1975; 1993; 1988; 1985; 2006) and Pierre Bourdieu (1983; 2007; 2012; 2013) to the body. It is based on a literature review that draws especially on Foucault's concepts of discipline and surveillance and on Bourdieu's concepts of doxa and symbolic violence. The aim is to support research on the body that treats the two authors' approaches as complementary.

Keywords: body; Bourdieu; Foucault; discipline; doxa.
