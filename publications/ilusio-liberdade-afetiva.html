A ilusio da liberdade afetiva: poliamor, amor romântico e classificações relacionais de gênero

The illusion of affective freedom: polyamory, romantic love, and relational gender classifications

Pedro Araujo and Lucas Flôres Vasques. Novos Olhares Sociais 9(1), 113–137, 2026.

PDF (537 KB) · https://doi.org/10.66983/nos.v7i1.5951

Abstract

This article analyzes how femininities and masculinities are produced, hierarchized, and reclassified within the discourses of polyamory practitioners. Based on qualitative research conducted in both digital and face-to-face environments (the Feeld application, Facebook groups, participant observation, digital ethnography, questionnaires, and semi-structured interviews), the text examines the tension between the polyamorous critique of romantic love and the persistence of gendered dispositions in the organization of desire, jealousy, affective responsibility, and recognition. The theoretical framework articulates Pierre Bourdieu’s relational sociology, specifically the notions of doxa, habitus, illusio, symbolic capital, symbolic violence, and masculine domination, with contributions from gender studies and the sociology of intimacy. It is argued that polyamory shifts the ethics of sexual fidelity toward an ethics of communication, negotiation, and transparency; however, it does not automatically eliminate the androcentric classifications that unevenly authorize male and female multiple desires. The contribution of this article lies in proposing the concept of affective gender classifications to understand the schemes that define who is allowed to desire, who must provide care, who needs to justify their freedom, and who is recognized as a legitimate loving subject.

Keywords: polyamory; gender; romantic love; masculinities; Pierre Bourdieu.
