A fotografia na Sociologia: cartografia da produção científica brasileira e internacional

Photography in Sociology: cartography of Brazilian and international scientific production

Lucas Flôres Vasques, Thaís Cristina Caetano de Souza and Luana Estela Di Pires. Novos Rumos Sociológicos 13(23), 5–30, 2025.

PDF (948 KB) · https://doi.org/10.15210/norus.v13i23.30297

Abstract

This article maps sociological research on photography in Brazil and internationally, drawing on bibliometric and lexical analyses carried out with IRaMuTeQ and VOSviewer. Using a corpus of articles indexed in SciELO and Web of Science, it examines the conceptual repertoires and thematic axes that structure the use of photography as an object and an instrument of research in the social sciences. The results show that Brazilian research is strongly anchored in a Bourdieusian framework, with an emphasis on theoretical and symbolic approaches, whereas international research stands out for its methodological and applied use of images, especially in studies of health, youth, gender and digital consumption. Photography thus appears as an arena of epistemological disputes, reflecting different ways of doing science and forms of consecration in the academic fields analysed. The article concludes that theoretical depth and methodology need to be brought together critically, in a visual sociology, or sociology of the image, that is reflexive, situated and attentive to contemporary changes in visual culture.
