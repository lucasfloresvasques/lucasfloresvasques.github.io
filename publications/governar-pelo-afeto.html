Governar pelo afeto: emoções e poder simbólico no discurso empresarial da revista Exame

Governing through affect: emotions and symbolic power in the business discourse of Exame magazine

Lucas Flôres Vasques

Journal
Estudos de Sociologia 30(3), 1283–1300, 2025.
DOI
10.52780/res.v30i3.20433

Abstract

This article analyses the rise of emotions in the Brazilian business world, taking as its empirical object 148 issues of the magazine Exame (1990–2007). Drawing on Pierre Bourdieu's relational sociology and Norbert Elias's figurational sociology, it examines how emotions, beyond subjectivity, are shaped into legitimate forms of distinction and symbolic consecration. Exame is analysed as an instance of social consecration that, by prescribing desirable emotional styles such as empathy, self-control and enthusiasm, establishes a “corporate emotional etiquette”. The study uses content analysis in MAXQDA, with a dictionary of terms and a coding of the compiled material. It shows that emotional management becomes a moral grammar that structures hierarchies in the business world.
