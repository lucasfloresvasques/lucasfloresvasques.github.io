I am a PhD candidate in Social Sciences at São Paulo State University (UNESP), in Araraquara, funded by FAPESP and supervised by Maria Aparecida Chaves Jardim. My thesis looks at how flowers and ornamental plants are classified and judged in the trade and by the people who grow them. I will defend it in March 2027.

FAPESP has funded my research since 2016. From March 2025 to March 2026 I was a visiting researcher at IDHE.S, ENS Paris-Saclay, working with Frédéric Lebaron. I use Geometric Data Analysis to construct social spaces.

My next project, a postdoctoral proposal at the Federal University of São Carlos with Silvio Eduardo Alvarez Candido, is about who makes federal environmental policy in Brazil.
