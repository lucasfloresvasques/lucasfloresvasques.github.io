PhD candidate in Social Sciences · UNESP · defence scheduled for March 2027

My research examines how social classifications, judgments, and positions shape markets, public policy, and cultural practices.

My PhD in Social Sciences at UNESP, supervised by Maria Aparecida Chaves Jardim, examines the classifications and judgments that confer value on flowers and ornamental plants, from the commercial standards of Veiling Holambra to the choices of those who grow them. The thesis, The Social Life of Flowers, will be defended in March 2027.

I have held FAPESP fellowships since 2016, in five successive schemes, from undergraduate research to the PhD and a research stay abroad. In 2025–2026 I was a visiting researcher at IDHE.S, ENS Paris-Saclay, supervised by Frédéric Lebaron. I work with Geometric Data Analysis in the tradition of Brigitte Le Roux and Henry Rouanet.

My next project, a postdoctoral proposal at the Federal University of São Carlos (UFSCar) supervised by Silvio Eduardo Alvarez Candido, compares the trajectories and positions of those who make federal environmental policy in the executive, Congress and the National Environment Council (CONAMA) between 2003 and 2026.

Research lines

Classification, value and markets
How standards, experts and institutions make a difference pertinent, from ornamental plants to the Brazilian Sustainable Taxonomy.
Elites, the state and environmental policy
Positions and position-takings of Latin American parliamentary elites and of those who make federal environmental policy in Brazil.
Taste, emotions and cultural inequalities
Repertoires of taste, emotions in economic life and inequalities in Internet use in Brazil.
Geometric Data Analysis
Specific MCA and class specific analysis (CSA), in the tradition of Le Roux and Rouanet.
