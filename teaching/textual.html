Teaching and materials

Text analysis with IRaMuTeQ and R

Course proposal, 12 hours.

To prepare a documented corpus and relate lexical results to the reading of the texts and to the research problem.

Audience: Researchers who work with interviews, documents or social media posts. The quanteda part requires basic R.

Outline

Building the corpus: sources, scope and textual unit. Metadata and preparation for IRaMuTeQ. Concordances and the lexical table. Correspondence analysis (CA) of a lexical table. Descending hierarchical classification (Reinert method) and similarity analysis. Returning to the texts and the limits of interpretation.

Programme

  1. Building the corpus Sources, scope, textual unit, metadata, duplicates and the conditions under which the texts circulate.
  2. Preparing for IRaMuTeQ UTF-8 encoding, text headers, checking the import and recording preparation decisions.
  3. Concordances and the lexical table Exploration in quanteda: words in context, selecting the vocabulary and the count matrix.
  4. CA and interpretation Planes, contributions and returning to the texts. How CA differs from the Reinert classification and from similarity analysis.

Assignment

Prepare synthetic texts for import and compare a concordance-based reading with an exploration of the lexical table. To hand in: the corpus, the code map and a note on the limits of the analysis.
