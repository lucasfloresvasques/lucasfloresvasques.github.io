Teaching and materials

Multiple correspondence analysis and the construction of social space

Course proposal, 12 hours.

To build an MCA specification and justify the choices of population, variables and categories before interpreting the factorial plane.

Audience: Graduate students and researchers with a basic grounding in data analysis who work with categorical variables.

Outline

Social space and geometric data analysis. Unit of analysis, population and sources. Codebook and coding of categories. Active and supplementary variables; passive categories and specific MCA. The cloud of categories and the cloud of individuals. Eigenvalues and modified rates, contributions and quality of representation. Supplementary variables and deviations between categories. Documenting the analysis.

Programme

  1. Building the problem and the dataset Unit of analysis, population, documentation of sources and the codebook.
  2. Coding and categories Active and supplementary variables, non-responses, low-frequency categories and passive categories in specific MCA.
  3. Geometry and reading the results Eigenvalues and modified rates, contributions and quality of representation; reading the planes and the tables together.
  4. Documenting an analysis Comparing specifications, writing the methodological note and keeping a record of versions.

Assignment

Audit a synthetic dataset, formulate two justified specifications and write an interpretive note that keeps the results apart from the analyst's choices.
