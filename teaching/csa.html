Teaching and materials

Comparing subclouds: class specific analysis in R and SPAD

Course proposal, 8 hours.

To distinguish a group's position in the reference space from the directions of variation specific to that group.

Audience: Researchers who already read MCA output and can tell coordinates, contributions and quality of representation apart.

Outline

Subclouds and the reference space. Defining the group and common coding. Class specific analysis (CSA) and specific MCA. Mean point, dispersion and orientation of the axes. Angles between the CSA axes and those of the reference MCA. Checking results between R (GDAtools) and SPAD.

Programme

  1. Reference cloud and subcloud Defining the group, the reference population, common coding and weights.
  2. Specification in GDAtools The speMCA() and csMCA() functions; estimation on the same dataset and a record of the options.
  3. Comparative reading Mean point, dispersion and orientation of the axes; telling where the group sits apart from how it is internally differentiated.
  4. R, SPAD and documentation Checking the tables and the metric before comparing maps; adapting the maps for SPAD.

Assignment

Define a subcloud in a synthetic dataset and write a comparison that does not confuse the group's mean point with its internal structure. The SPAD part requires a licensed copy of the program.

Readings

  • Le Roux, Brigitte; Rouanet, Henry (2010). Multiple Correspondence Analysis. Thousand Oaks: Sage.
  • Robette, Nicolas. GDAtools, R package: csMCA() documentation.
