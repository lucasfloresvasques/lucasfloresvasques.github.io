Courses I can teach

  • Multiple correspondence analysis and the construction of social space12 hours. To build an MCA specification and justify the choices of population, variables and categories before interpreting the factorial plane.

  • Comparing subclouds: class specific analysis in R and SPAD8 hours. To distinguish a group's position in the reference space from the directions of variation specific to that group.

  • Text analysis with IRaMuTeQ and R12 hours. To prepare a documented corpus and relate lexical results to the reading of the texts and to the research problem.
